A SIMPLE EXPLANATION

How does this work?

We encrypt your secret, then tuck it into tiny image details that are too subtle to notice. Think of changing one pixel’s color by just one step:

100→101

The color barely changes. That single-bit difference can carry data.

EncryptionPBKDF2 turns your password into an AES-256 key. AES-GCM detects tampering and wrong passwords.
Image safetyPixels survive only if the image is preserved exactly. Compression, resizing, screenshots and filters destroy the hidden data.
PrivacyEverything runs locally in your browser. This tool does not send your files or password to a server.
!
Keep the original quality

Always use the downloaded PNG. Sharing through WhatsApp, Instagram or email may recompress it and remove the secret.

Audio & video files

They are not supported as carriers yet. Appending data to them is unreliable; re-encoding usually strips it. Use a PNG image instead.
